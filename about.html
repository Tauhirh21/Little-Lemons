Our Story

Small batches. Real fruit. No shortcuts.

How it started

Little Lemons began in a small home kitchen with a hand juicer, a crate of lemons, and a stubborn belief that real lemonade tastes better than anything you can buy in a plastic bottle. What started as a weekend batch for friends quickly turned into a weekly order list — and we haven't looked back since.

What makes us different

  • Real fruit, always. Every bottle is made with freshly squeezed lemons — no powders, no concentrates.
  • Small batches. We make lemonade in 20-bottle batches so every one tastes the same: perfect.
  • Simple ingredients. Lemons, sugar, water, and whatever fruit the flavor calls for. That's it.
  • Locally sourced. We buy our lemons and berries from local farmers wherever possible.

Our four flavors

We keep our menu small on purpose. Four flavors, each perfected over dozens of batches: Classic for purists, Mint for something cooler, Pink for a berry twist, and Passion for a tropical kick. Every bottle is dated by hand and best enjoyed within 5 days.
